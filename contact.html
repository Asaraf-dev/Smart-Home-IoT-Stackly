SMART HOME BASICS

A smart home uses connected devices and automation to help manage features such as lighting, climate, and security. The available functions depend on the devices and platform you choose.

You can explore product categories such as smart lighting, security devices, climate controls, sensors, and automation solutions. Check each product's specifications before choosing a device.

Explore Products

Compatibility depends on your existing devices, communication protocols, network, and any required hub. Review the manufacturer's specifications or contact us before purchasing.

Requirements vary by device. Some local functions may work without internet, while remote access, cloud features, or voice assistants may require an internet connection.
